# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Property owners and managers who operate short-term rentals. They use the application to manage properties, reservations, guests, payments, expenses, occupancy, and check-in/check-out workflows.

## Product Purpose

Apartamentos Valeria centralizes day-to-day rental operations. It should make the current state of reservations, occupancy, collections, and property activity immediately understandable and keep financial and operational records consistent.

## Brand Personality

Clear, restrained, and operational. The interface should feel dependable and calm while preserving the existing Kanagawa visual identity.

## Anti-references

Avoid ambiguous controls, decorative complexity, hidden state transitions, and interfaces that make financial or reservation status depend on unclear side effects.

## Design Principles

- Make operational state visible without requiring extra actions.
- Keep reservation lifecycle and payment lifecycle conceptually separate.
- Prefer explicit workflows over editable status fields with unclear consequences.
- Preserve established visual conventions unless a usability problem requires change.
- Present occupancy and financial information with consistent definitions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic controls, and reduced-motion support. User-facing copy remains in Spanish (AR).
